# 🔴 NISMO EV

A clean, fast EV charging calculator built for Nissan Ariya owners and other electric vehicles.

## Features

- ⚡ Level 1, Level 2, and DC Fast Charging calculations
- 🔋 Battery percentage to percentage charging estimates
- ⏱️ Charging time calculator
- 💲 Charging cost calculator
- 📈 Estimated driving range added
- 🕒 Finish time estimator
- ⛽ EV vs. Gas trip cost comparison
- 🚗 Nissan Ariya 87 kWh preset
- 📱 Mobile-friendly design

## Supported Vehicles

Currently includes:

- Nissan Ariya Empower+ (87 kWh)

Additional vehicles will be added in future updates.

## Roadmap

- Battery degradation tracker
- FPL Time-of-Use electricity rates
- Public charging cost comparison
- Route planning
- Charging history
- Battery health estimator
- Multi-vehicle garage
- Dark/Light themes

## Disclaimer

All charging times and costs are estimates. Actual results depend on charger output, battery temperature, battery state of charge, weather, charging efficiency, and vehicle charging behavior.
